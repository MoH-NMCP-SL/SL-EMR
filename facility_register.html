What this register does

  • Master list and map holds the official facility list, filtered and mapped.
  • Add facility submits a new facility for the admin to accept or reject.
  • Register shows what this office has submitted and its status.
  • Bulk upload loads many facilities from a spreadsheet at once.
  • Admin accepts, rejects, updates the level, status and codes of a facility, and pushes accepted facilities to DHIS2.
  • A facility with no coordinates cannot appear on a map, so add them where they are missing.
